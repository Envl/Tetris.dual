this is a tetris game that supports dual mode which is similar to push mode in Tetris DS on Nintendo DS. in dual mode, 2 players share the same board but each player has their own territory on the board. when one player clears lines, it shifts the whole board towards the opponent's side, making it harder for the opponent to play. the shared board is 44 rows height, at the beginning of the game, there are 2 obstacles in the middle of the board, to let tetris to drop on. the game overs when locked pieces reach the top of either player's territory.
